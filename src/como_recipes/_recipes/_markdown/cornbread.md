# Cornbread

Tags: American



## Ingredients

1/4 cupbutter

1/6 cupsugar

1 largeegg

1/2 cupbuttermilk

1/4 tsp.baking soda

1/2 cupcornmeal

1/2 cupflour

1/4 tsp.salt



## Instructions

Preheat to 375 F. Melt butter. Stir in sugar. Add eggs, beat.

Combine buttermilk and soda, stir into mixture.

Add remaining ingredients, stir until blended.

Bake for about 20 minutes, until slightly brown on top.
