# Sloppy Joe

Tags: American



## Ingredients

1 batchof Kaiser Rolls

1 lbground beef or Boca crumbles

1/4 cupred onion

1/2 tsp.garlic powder

2 tsp.yellow mustard

1/2 cupketchup

3 tsp.brown sugar

1/2 tbsp.molasses

1/4 tsp.salt and pepper



## Instructions

Brown meat. Mix spices. Simmer until good consistency.
