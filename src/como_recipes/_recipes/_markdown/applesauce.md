# Applesauce

Tags: American



## Ingredients

4 largeapples

3/4 cupwater

1/16 cupwhite sugar

1/16 cupbrown sugar

1/2 tsp.ground cinnamon



## Instructions

Peel and core apples.

Combine everything and cook over medium heat for 15-20 minutes (until apples are soft).

Allow to cool, then mash with fork or potato masher.
