# Banana Bread

Tags: American



## Ingredients

1/4 cupbutter

1/8 cupsugar

1 largeegg

1 ripebanana

1/8 tsp.of salt

1/2 tsp.baking soda

1/2 cupflour



## Instructions

Preheat oven to 300 F.

Cream butter and sugar.

Combine all dry ingredients and mix well.

Combine all remaining ingredients and mix well.

Bake for 35 minutes.
