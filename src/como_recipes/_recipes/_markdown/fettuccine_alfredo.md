# Fettuccine Alfredo

Tags: Italian



## Ingredients

3 eggsworth of fettuccine noodles

1/4 cupbutter

1 cupheavy cream

3 clovescrushed garlic

1 1/2cups freshly grated Parmesan

1/2 tbsp.fresh parsley



## Instructions

Melt butter in saucepan over medium heat.

Add cream and garlic, simmer for 5 minutes.

Add most of the cheese, whisk quickly.

Add rest of cheese just before serving.

Garnish with the parsley if desired.
