# Red Beans

Tags: American



## Ingredients

1 recipesworth of rice

1 tbsp.olive oil

4 clovesgarlic

1/3 whiteonion

1 stalkcelery

2 bayleaves

1/2 tsp.cayenne pepper

1 tsp.thyme

1/8 tsp.sage

1 tbsp.parsley

1 tsp.Cajun seasoning

1 cupwater



## Instructions

Make rice. Mix spices together.

Carefully caramelize garlic, onion, and celery in oil.

Add water to onions and garlic, then mix in spices.

Add beans and any other add-ins, simmer for at least 15 minutes.

Serve on rice.
