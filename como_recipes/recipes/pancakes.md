# Pancakes (American)

## Ingredients

1/2  cup flour 

1  tbsp. sugar 

1/2  tsp. baking powder 

1/4  tsp. baking soda 

1/4  tsp. salt 

1/2  egg 

1  tbsp. butter 

1/2  cup buttermilk 




## Instructions

Combine dry ingredients.

Melt butter, mix with egg and buttermilk; whisk well.

Cook on griddle.
