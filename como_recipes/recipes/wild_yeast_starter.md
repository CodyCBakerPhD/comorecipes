# Wild Yeast Starter (American)

## Ingredients

1/4 cup organic hard red wheat flour

1/4 cup filtered water

## Instructions

Day 1: Mix together 1/4 cup water and 1/4 cup flour. Pour into a clean mason jar and cover with a thin cloth and rubber band. Place in a cool, shady place outside, near a tree or bush.

Day 2: Add 1/4 cup water and 1/4 cup flour to mixture. Place back outside.

Day 3-9: Repeat Day 2 instructions.

Day 10: Add 1/4 cup flour and 1/4 cup water to mixture. Pour into a clean jar and place in fridge.

Day 11-16: Repeat Day 10 instructions, using the same jar.

Day 17: Ready to use.
