# Chocolate Cake (American)

## Ingredients

Batter:

1 cup flour

1 cup sugar

3/8 cup cocoa powder

1 tsp. baking powder

3/4 tsp. baking soda

1/2 tsp. salt

1/2 tsp. espresso powder

1/2 cup milk

1/4 cup vegetable oil

1 egg

1 tsp. vanilla

1/2 cup boiling water

Icing:

11/4 cup sifted powdered sugar

6 tbsp. cocoa powder

6 tbsp. warmed butter

5 tbsp. evaporated milk

1 tsp. vanilla extract


## Instructions

Batter: Preheat over to 350 F. Spray pan. Add all dry ingredients and mix. Boil water. Add non-water wet ingredients and mix until well-combined. Reduce speed and carefully add boiling water and mix well. Immediately transfer mixture to pan and bake for 30-35 minutes.

Icing: Sift the cocoa powder and powdered sugar together. In separate bowl, cream butter until smooth, gradually beat in sugar mixture alternately with evaporated milk. Blend in vanila. Beat until light and fluffy.
