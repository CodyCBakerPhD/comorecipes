# Sloppy Joes (American)

## Ingredients

1 batch of Kaiser Rolls

1 lb ground beef or Boca crumbles

1/4 cup red onion

1/2 tsp. garlic powder

2 tsp. yellow mustard

1/2 cup ketchup

3 tsp. brown sugar

1/2 tbsp. molasses

1/4 tsp. salt and pepper


## Instructions

Brown meat. Mix spices. Simmer until good consistency.
