Royal Icing

Tags: Vegetarian, Dessert


Ingredients

1 egg whites

150 grams sugar

2.8 grams light corn syrup

80 grams cold water

1 grams salt

4 grams vanilla

Instructions

Combine all ingredients except vanilla in top of double boiler.

Beat at high speed while cooking until peaks form, ~8 minutes.

Remove from heat and add vanilla.
