Rice

  • American
  • Vegetarian
  • Side

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Thoroughly rinse and pre-soak rice for 15 minutes.
  2. Add everything to a covered pot, bring to boil over medium-high heat (takes around 3 minutes).
  3. Immediately reduce to second lowest heat level for around 20 minutes.
  4. When water is evaporated, check rice to ensure it's not crunchy or soggy.
