Sweet Fire Rub

Tags: American, Vegetarian, Spicy


Ingredients

1/2 cup brown sugar

1/2 cup white sugar

38 grams paprika

1 tbsp onion powder

10 grams garlic powder

8 grams chili powder

1 tbsp cayenne pepper

2 tbsp salt & pepper

Instructions

Mix together and store in pantry.
