Roast Pumpkin

  • American
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Cut pumpkin in half length-wise. Scrape out seeds and string.
  2. Brush with oil and pierce outer skin a few times for steam to escape. Bake for 45-50 minutes at 350 F. Let cool and then scoop out meat.
