Sweet Dough

Tags: American, Dessert, Vegetarian


Ingredients

166 grams whole milk

65 grams sugar

5 grams yeast

2 eggs , room temperature

330 grams flour

6 grams salt

113 grams butter


Notes

Butter should be room temperature; take out well in advance.

Instructions

Heat milk to 110 F in small saucepan over medium heat.

Stir in 1 tbsp sugar and yeast.

Let sit for 5 minutes.

Add eggs.

Combine remaining dry ingredients then knead into dough.

Let prove for 90 minutes.
