Banana Bread

Tags: American, Vegetarian, Fruit


Ingredients

57 grams butter

25 grams sugar

1 egg

1 overripe banana

1 grams salt

3 grams baking soda

75 grams flour

Instructions

Preheat oven to 300 F.

Cream butter and sugar.

Mix in banana and egg.

Combine all dry ingredients and mix well.

Combine all remaining ingredients and mix well.

Bake for 35 minutes.
