Enchilada Beef

Tags: Mexican, Entree


Ingredients

1 (24 oz.) chuck roast

enough olive oil

24 grams flour

4 grams salt and pepper

240 grams water

15 grams beef concentrate

Instructions

Dredge meat in flour and pepper. Brown in skillet.

Heat water to simmer, add beef concentrate.

Combine in crockpot, set to high for 1 hour. Turn to low for 6-7 hours.
