Spaghetti Squash

Tags: Italian, Vegetarian, Entree, Pasta


Ingredients

1 spaghetti squash

14 grams olive oil

4 grams salt and pepper

Instructions

Cut and clean inside of squash.

Coat with oil and season.

Place face down on baking sheet lined with aluminum foil.

Bake for 40 minutes at 375 F.
