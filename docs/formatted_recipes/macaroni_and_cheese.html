Macaroni And Cheese

  • American
  • Vegetarian
  • Entree
  • Pasta

Ingredients

Notes

  • Any combination of between 2% milk (for thin consistency) and heavy cream (very thick texture) may be used.

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta ahead of time. Cut 75% of cheese into small cubes or shred. Shred remaining 25%.
  2. Melt butter and slowly whisk in flour. Do not cook too much.
  3. Slowly whisk in milk or cream. When mixture is warm, thoroughly melt 75% of cheese.
  4. Season.
  5. Mix pasta into mixture. Layer into dish, sprinkling remaining 25% of cheese across layers, leaving some for the top.
  6. Bake for 15 minutes at 325 F; optionally, broil the top for added flavor and texture.
