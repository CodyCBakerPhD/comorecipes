Sloppy Joe

Tags: American, Vegetarian, Entree


Ingredients

1 Kaiser Rolls

453 grams ground beef or Boca crumbles

1/4 red onion

2 grams garlic powder

10 grams yellow mustard

120 grams ketchup

4 grams brown sugar

10 grams molasses

1 grams salt and pepper

Instructions

Brown meat. Mix spices. Simmer until good consistency.
