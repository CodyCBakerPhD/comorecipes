Meatloaf

  • American
  • Entree

Ingredients

Notes

  • Maybe try wrapping around a nice cheese?
  • Also maybe deep frying with crust?

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 360 F.
  2. Caramelize onions, adding garlic towards the end.
  3. Combine all dry ingredients.
  4. Add wet ingredients and meat, mix just to combine.
  5. Shape into loaf on a baking sheet.
  6. Bake for 40 minutes.
  7. Add glaze and bake for another 20 minutes.
  8. Rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
