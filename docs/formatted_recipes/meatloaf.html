Meatloaf

Tags: American, Entree


Ingredients

enough olive oil

80 grams white onion

8 grams minced garlic

17 grams ketchup

6 grams fresh parsley

110 grams Panko breadcrumbs

38 grams milk

3 grams salt & pepper

1 grams italian seasoning

1 85% lean ground beef (1 lb.)

1 Meatloaf glaze


Notes

Maybe try wrapping around a nice cheese?

Also maybe deep frying with crust?

Instructions

Preheat oven to 360 F.

Caramelize onions, adding garlic towards the end.

Combine all dry ingredients.

Add wet ingredients and meat, mix just to combine.

Shape into loaf on a baking sheet.

Bake for 40 minutes.

Add glaze and bake for another 20 minutes.

Rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
