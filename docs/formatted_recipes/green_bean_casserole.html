Green Bean Casserole

  • American
  • Side
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Drain green beans. Mix soup, milk, pepper in a bowl. Add beans and mix together. Add half of the onions, give a quick stir.
  2. Place in casserole dish and bake for 25 minutes at 350 F. Add remaining onions to top and bake another 5 minutes, keeping close watch for burning.
