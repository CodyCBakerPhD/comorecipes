Buttermilk Waffles

Tags: American, Breakfast, Vegetarian


Ingredients

35 grams butter, melted and cooled

1 egg

112 grams buttermilk

1 grams vanilla

60 grams flour

3 grams baking powder

2 grams baking soda

2 grams sugar

1 grams salt

12 grams pearl sugar


Notes

Waffle will be best if all ingredients are at room temperature.

Instructions

Heat waffle iron.

In a large bowl, combine all dry ingredients.

Create a depression for the buttermilk mixture.

Beat the eggs in a large measuring cup until frothy.

Add vanilla extract, buttermilk, and cooled melted butter.

Beat until well combined.

Pour into the depression and stir quickly but gently with a wooden spoon.

Gently incorporate pearl sugar.

Use about 1/4 of batter per waffle.

Cook until steam stops rising from the iron.
