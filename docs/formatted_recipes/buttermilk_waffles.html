Buttermilk Waffles

  • American
  • Breakfast
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

Notes

  • Waffle will be best if all ingredients are at room temperature.

Instructions

  1. Heat waffle iron.
  2. In a large bowl, combine all dry ingredients.
  3. Create a depression for the buttermilk mixture.
  4. Beat the eggs in a large measuring cup until frothy.
  5. Add vanilla extract, buttermilk, and cooled melted butter.
  6. Beat until well combined.
  7. Pour into the depression and stir quickly but gently with a wooden spoon.
  8. Gently incorporate pearl sugar.
  9. Use about a quarter of batter per waffle.
  10. Cook until steam stops rising from the iron.
