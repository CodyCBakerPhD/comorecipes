Hot Fudge

  • American
  • Dessert

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Whisk all ingredients except vanilla and butter thoroughly in a saucepan.
  2. Bring to boil for 3 minutes, stirring constantly.
  3. Achieve a temperature of 220 F, then remove from heat.
  4. Add vanilla and butter and stir until smooth.
  5. Keep in sealed container, reheating as necessary.
