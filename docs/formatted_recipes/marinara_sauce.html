Marinara Sauce

  • Component

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Warm tomatoes with seasoning in large pot.
  2. Caramelize onion in small saucepan.
  3. Add garlic towards the end.
  4. Deglaze pan with wine.
  5. Add mixture to tomato.
  6. Thin with water.
  7. Cook for an hour, stirring every 10 minutes.
