Ratatouille

  • French
  • Vegetarian
  • Entree

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Make piperade sauce ahead of time.
  2. Recommend using mandolin for slicing main vegetables. Wear safety gloves!
  3. Preheat to 325 F.
  4. On top of sauce, assemble sliced vegetables in artistic fashion. Drizzle with olive oil.
  5. Bake for ~45 minutes.
  6. Remember, anyone can cook!
