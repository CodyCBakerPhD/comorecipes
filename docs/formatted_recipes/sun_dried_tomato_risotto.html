Sun Dried Tomato Risotto

  • European
  • Entree

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 450 F.
  2. Bring pot of broth to a simmer.
  3. Toss zucchini and fresh tomatoes with a bit of oil and half of spice.
  4. Roast for 15 minutes.
  5. In a large risotto pan, saute shallots then garlic over medium heat.
  6. Add sun dried tomatoes and rice, stirring often.
  7. Add a ladle of broth and stir until absorbed.
  8. Repeat until rice is cooked, ~25 minutes.
  9. Lower heat to medium low and add cream sauce and remaining spice.
  10. Add salt, pepper, butter, and Parmesan.
  11. Combine with roasted vegetables.
