Broccoli Cheddar Soup

  • American
  • Vegetarian
  • Entree
  • Soup

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. In pot, melt butter over medium heat.
  2. Cook onion until softened.
  3. Stir in broccoli and cover with broth.
  4. Simmer until tender, 10-15 minutes.
  5. Mix in milk and garlic powder.
  6. Reduce heat and stir in cheese cubes until melted.
  7. In a small bowl, stir cornstarch into water until dissolved.
  8. Stir mixture into soup, cook, stirring frequently, until thick.
