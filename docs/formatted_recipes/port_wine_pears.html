Port Wine Pears

  • European
  • Dessert

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients except pears in a saucepan.
  2. Simmer gently for 7 minutes.
  3. Peel and core pears.
  4. Drop pears into liquid and simmer for at least 45 minutes, turning every so often, until they are very soft.
