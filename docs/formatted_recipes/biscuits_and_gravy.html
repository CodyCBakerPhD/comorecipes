Biscuits and Gravy

Tags: American, Entree, Vegetarian


Ingredients

1 biscuits

60 grams water

2 grams no chicken boullion

0.5 mushrooms

55 grams butter

30 grams onion

15 grams flour

175 grams milk

3.5 grams salt and pepper

0.25 grams dried rosemary

Instructions

Bake biscuits.

Prepare vegetable stock.

Sautee onions and mushrooms in butter.

Sprinkle with flour
