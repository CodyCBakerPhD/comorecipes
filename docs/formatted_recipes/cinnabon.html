Cinnabon

  • American
  • Dessert

Ingredients

Notes

  • Reheat for 30 seconds in microwave.

Instructions

  1. Make dough.
  2. Make filling.
  3. When ready, roll very flat to about 15 x 20 inch shape.
  4. Coat with filling.
  5. Roll and cut into pieces.
  6. Proof for 30 minutes.
  7. Preheat oven to 400 F.
  8. Bake for 15 minutes.
  9. Ice when cool.
