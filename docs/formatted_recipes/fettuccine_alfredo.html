Instructions

  1. Melt butter in saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Add cream and garlic, simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Add most of the cheese, whisk quickly.
  4. Add rest of cheese just before serving.
  5. Garnish with the parsley if desired.
