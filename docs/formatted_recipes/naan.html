Naan

Tags: Indian, Vegetarian, Side


Ingredients

3 grams yeast

12 grams sugar

120 grams warm water

10 grams milk

1 egg

28 grams butter

200 grams bread flour

3 grams salt

enough ghee

Instructions

Activate yeast.

Melt butter and mix with milk.

Whisk in egg.

Separately combine flour and salt.

Combine all wet ingredients with dry ingredients.

Mix well and leave to rise for 1 hour.

Divide into 8 evenly sized pieces.

Proof for 15 minutes.

Roll flat and cook in a hot wok or skillet.

Brush tops with ghee.
