Cauliflower Bites

Tags: Vegetarian, Entree, Spicy


Ingredients

90 grams flour

30 grams cornstarch

3 grams salt and pepper

2 grams garlic powder

240 grams water

1 cauliflower

120 grams hot sauce

57 grams melted butter

Instructions

Preheat oven to 450 F.

Line baking sheet with foil.

Whisk flour, cornstarch, salt, pepper, and garlic powder in a bowl.

Whisk in water until smooth.

Add cauliflower and toss to coat.

Bake 20 minutes or until lightly browned.

Combine hot sauce and butter in a bowl.

Toss cauliflower in sauce and bake another 5 minutes to seal glaze.
