Cauliflower Bites

  • Vegetarian
  • Entree
  • Spicy

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 450 F.
  2. Line baking sheet with foil.
  3. Whisk flour, cornstarch, salt, pepper, and garlic powder in a bowl.
  4. Whisk in water until smooth.
  5. Add cauliflower and toss to coat.
  6. Bake 25 minutes or until lightly browned.
  7. Combine hot sauce and butter in a small saucepan; heat until melted together.
  8. Toss cauliflower in sauce and bake another 5 minutes to seal glaze.
