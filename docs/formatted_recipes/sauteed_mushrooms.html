Sauteed Mushrooms

Tags: American, Side, Vegetarian


Ingredients

42 grams olive oil

42 grams butter

1 whole mushrooms

5 grams garlic

15 grams red wine

3 grams salt and pepper

Instructions

Heat oil and butter in large saucepan over medium heat.

Cook all dry ingredients until mushrooms are lightly browned.

Deglaze pan with wine, reduce heat to low, and simmer until mushrooms are tender, ~6 minutes.
