Pancakes

Tags: American, Breakfast, Vegetarian


Ingredients

120 grams flour

2 tbsp sugar

1 tsp baking powder

1/2 tsp baking soda

1/2 tsp salt

1 portions egg

2 tbsp butter

1 cup buttermilk

Instructions

Combine dry ingredients.

Melt butter, mix with egg and buttermilk; whisk well.

Cook on griddle.
