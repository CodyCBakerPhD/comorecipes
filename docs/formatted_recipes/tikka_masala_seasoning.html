Tikka Masala Seasoning

Tags: Component


Ingredients

100 grams garam masala

30 grams cumin

20 grams coriander

5 grams ginger

80 grams salt and pepper

100 grams red pepper flakes


Notes

Makes about enough for 10 portions of Tikka Masala.

Instructions

Mix and store in airtight container.
