Frittata

  • Vegetarian
  • Breakfast
  • Entree

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400 F.
  2. Spread mushrooms over a pan with some oil and salt.
  3. Roast for 15 minutes.
  4. Heat some more oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  5. Saute onion.
  6. Add kale, season with a bit of salt, and cook until wilted.
  7. Add tomatoes and cook until soft.
  8. Add in mushrooms.
  9. Preheat boiler.
  10. Crack and whisk eggs, season with a bit of salt and pepper.
  11. Add to skillet and gently incorporate.
  12. Cook until broiler for about 3 minutes.
  13. Sprinkle top with cheese and broil another 3 minutes.
