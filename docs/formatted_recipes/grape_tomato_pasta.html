Grape Tomato Pasta

Tags: Italian, Vegetarian, Entree


Ingredients

1 fresh angel hair pasta

enough olive oil

8 grams garlic

298 grams grape tomatoes

3 grams salt

2 grams crushed red pepper

5 grams sliced fresh basil

30 grams lemon juice


Notes

Some seared scallops on the side are a great addition to this dish.

Instructions

Cook pasta.

Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.

Add garlic and cook while stirring for ~1 minute.

Add grape tomatoes, salt, and crushed red pepper.

Cook until tomatoes are soft and start to burst, ~6 minutes.

Remove from heat and stir in basil and lemon juice.

Toss with cooked pasta and serve.
