Grape Tomato Pasta

  • Italian
  • Vegetarian
  • Entree

Ingredients

Notes

  • Some seared scallops on the side are a great addition to this dish.

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add garlic and cook while stirring for ~1 minute.
  4. Add grape tomatoes, salt, and crushed red pepper.
  5. Cook until tomatoes are soft and start to burst, ~6 minutes.
  6. Remove from heat and stir in basil and lemon juice.
  7. Toss with cooked pasta and serve.
