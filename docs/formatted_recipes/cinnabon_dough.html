Cinnabon Dough

Tags: American, Dessert


Ingredients

1 cinnabon dough

1 cinnabon filling

1 cinnabon icing


Notes

Reheat for 30 seconds in microwave.

Instructions

Make dough.

Make filling.

When ready, roll very flat to about 15 x 20 inch shape.

Coat with filling.

Roll and cut into pieces.

Proof for 30 minutes.

Preheat oven to 400 F.

Bake for 15 minutes.

Ice when cool.
