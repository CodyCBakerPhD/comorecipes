Cherry Pie Filling

  • American
  • Dessert
  • Vegetarian
  • Fruit

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Place cherries into a saucepan over medium heat and cover.
  2. Heat cherries until they release their juices and simmer 10 to 15 minutes.
  3. Stir often.
  4. In a bowl, whisk sugar with cornstarch until smooth.
  5. Pour mixture into cherries and juice and combine.
  6. Return to low heat, bring to simmer, and cook until filling has thickened (about 2 minutes).
  7. Remove from heat and use as pie filling.
  8. Cook to pie crust instructions.
