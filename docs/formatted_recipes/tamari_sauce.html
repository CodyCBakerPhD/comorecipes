Instructions

  1. Mix water and cornstarch to make a slurry.
  2. Heat oil in saucepan. Add everything except cornstarch mixture, cook for 2 minutes.
  3. Add cornstarch mixture to thicken.
  4. Season with spices, cook until desired consistency.
