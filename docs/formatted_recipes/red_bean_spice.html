Red Bean Spice

Tags: Component


Ingredients

10 grams cayenne pepper

10 grams thyme

1 grams sage

15 grams parsley

40 grams Cajun seasoning


Notes

Makes enough for about 10 portions of red beans.

Instructions

Make rice. Mix spices together.

Carefully caramelize garlic, onion, and celery in oil.

Add water to onions and garlic, then mix in spices.

Add beans and any other add-ins, simmer for at least 15 minutes.

Serve on rice.
