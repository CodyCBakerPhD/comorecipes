Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Brown pork with dry spices rubbed in.
  2. Slow cook in other ingredients for 6-8 hours on low in a small slow-cooker.
  3. Remove and shred, reserving strained liquid.
  4. Crisp in large skillet with remaining juices until evaporated.
