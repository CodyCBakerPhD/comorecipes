Baked Potato

  • American
  • Entree
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Use aluminum foil for easy cleanup.
  2. Thoroughly scrub potatoes.
  3. Cut into quarters lengthwise.
  4. Rub all over with peanut oil and coat with a bit of large-grain salt.
  5. Bake for 45 min at 425 F.
