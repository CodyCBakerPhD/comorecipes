Sauteed Green Beans

  • American
  • Side
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Coat the bottom of a cast iron skillet with olive oil. Warm oil at medium heat.
  2. Add green beans, stirring to coat with olive oil.
  3. Add salt and pepper to taste, and stir again.
  4. Cover cast iron skillet with a lid, and cook for approximately 8 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. When green beans are crisping on the outside, reduce heat to low and continue cooking for ~5 minutes.
