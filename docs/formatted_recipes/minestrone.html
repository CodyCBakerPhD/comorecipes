Minestrone

  • Italian
  • Vegetarian
  • Entree
  • Soup

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Saute onions and carrots in large pot with olive oil until soft.
  2. Add garlic and saute for 1 minute.
  3. Deglaze pot with red wine.
  4. Add broth, water, and seasoning.
  5. Bring to boil.
  6. Add beans, tomatoes, zucchini, pasta, and green beans.
  7. Simmer for at least 5 minutes.
  8. Add pasta and simmer until pasta is almost cooked.
  9. Add spinach and simmer until tender.
