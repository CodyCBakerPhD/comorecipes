Black Bean Chili

Tags: American, Vegetarian, Entree, Soup, Spicy


Ingredients

20 grams garlic

125 grams minced white onion

1 fresh tomatoes

40 grams black bean chili spice

200 grams dried black beans

enough peanut oil

Instructions

Carefully caramelize onions and garlic.

Add tomatoes.

Mix all dry ingredients separately and add to the mixture.

Add beans and some extra oil.

Stir and simmer for at least 70 minutes.
