Buffalo Wings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat oil to 375 F.
  2. Fry chicken wings for ~10 minutes.
  3. Drain and then toss with salt and pepper.
  4. In a wok, melt butter then add other wet ingredients.
  5. Reduce slightly then toss chicken wings in mixture.
