Eggplant Parmesan

  • Italian
  • Vegetarian
  • Entree

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.
  2. Slice eggplant into 1/2 inch rounds.
  3. Beat egg in a bowl.
  4. Dip eggplant slices in egg, then coat in bread crumbs.
  5. Place on a baking sheet and bake for 5 minutes.
  6. Flip eggplant slices and bake for another 5 minutes.
  7. Spread marinara sauce on the bottom of a baking dish.
  8. Layer eggplant slices and top with cheese.
  9. Bake ~25 minutes, until cheese is melted.
