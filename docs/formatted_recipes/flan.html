Flan

  • Vegetarian
  • Dessert

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350 F.
  2. Prepare a water bath around the ramekins.
  3. Blend eggs, milks, salt, and vanilla.
  4. Blend until very smooth, ~30 seconds; avoid frothing.
  5. In a medium saucepan, combine sugar, water, and salt.
  6. Cook over medium-low heat until sugar is dissolved.
  7. Increase heat to high and cook until sugar is lightly colored, ~5 minutes.
  8. Immediately lower heat and continue cooking to golden.
  9. Immediately pour caramel into ramekins.
  10. Allow caramel to set for 5 minutes.
  11. Bake for 40 minutes, until slightly jiggly but firm.
  12. Cool for 30 minutes then chill for at least 4 hours.
