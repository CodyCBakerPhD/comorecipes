Flan

Tags: Vegetarian, Dessert


Ingredients

4 egg

2 egg yolks

3 grams salt

397 grams condensed milk

340 grams evaporated milk

13 grams vanilla

200 grams sugar

60 grams water

1 grams salt

Instructions

Preheat oven to 350 F.

Prepare a water bath around the ramekins.

Blend eggs, milks, salt, and vanilla.

Blend until very smooth, ~30 seconds; avoid frothing.

In a medium saucepan, combine sugar, water, and salt.

Cook over medium-low heat until sugar is dissolved.

Increase heat to high and cook until sugar is lightly colored, ~5 minutes.

Immediately lower heat and continue cooking to golden.

Immediately pour caramel into ramekins.

Allow caramel to set for 5 minutes.

Bake for 40 minutes, until slightly jiggly but firm.

Cool for 30 minutes then chill for at least 4 hours.
