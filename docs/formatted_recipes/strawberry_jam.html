Strawberry Jam

  • American
  • Dessert
  • Vegetarian
  • Fruit

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Carefully weigh the white and brown sugar into a small bowl.
  2. Save adding honey this mixture until just ready to add to strawberries.
  3. Heat strawberries slowly over medium-low heat stirring constantly, until juices begin leaking.
  4. Then quickly incorporate the sugars and cornstarch and stir well until everything is homogeneous.
  5. Increase heat to just above medium, and reach a boil stirring often.
  6. When mixture is at boil (it remains boiling upon stirring) then stir constantly for one minute and then remove from heat.
  7. Let cool before using.
