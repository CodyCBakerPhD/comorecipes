Caramel

Tags: American, Vegetarian, Dessert


Ingredients

200 grams sugar

246 grams dark corn syrup

113 grams butter

115 grams cream

2 grams vanilla

Instructions

Combine all ingredients except vanilla in a saucepan.

Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture reaches 248 F.

Remove from heat and stir in vanilla.

Pour into a greased pan and let cool.
