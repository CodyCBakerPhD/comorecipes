Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients except vanilla in a saucepan.
  2. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture reaches 248 F.
  3. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla.
  4. Pour into a greased pan and let cool.
