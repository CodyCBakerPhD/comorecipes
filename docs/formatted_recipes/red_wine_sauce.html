Red Wine Sauce

  • Italian
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat oil over medium-high heat. Cook garlic for ~30 seconds. Add wine and reach a simmer.
  2. Reduce to half, about 2-3 minutes. Add water and soy sauce. Reduce to half again.
  3. Reduce heat to medium-low and whisk in butter, 1 tbsp at a time. Stir in parsley and serve.
