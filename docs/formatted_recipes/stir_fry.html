Stir Fry

Tags: Asian, Vegetarian, Entree


Ingredients

1 rice recipe

enough peanut oil

1 tofu, beef, chicken, pork, or shrimp

1 shallots

10 grams garlic

100 grams carrots

1 broccoli

1 cauliflower

1 cabbage

100 grams green beans

1 pea pods

30 grams spinach

1 water chestnut

Instructions

For meat; cut into thin strips and fry in separate pan without onions or garlic.

For tofu; get extra-firm and deep fry covered in cornstarch.

In the main woc, fry vegetables in order of cooking time (roughly given in order of ingredients).

Add sauce and garnish with meat or tofu.
