Chocolate Ganache

  • American
  • Dessert
  • Vegetarian
  • Chocolate

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Combine chocolate and heavy cream in a small saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Stir frequently until chocolate is melted and mixture is smooth.
  3. Remove from heat, stir in vanilla extract.
  4. Allow chocolate to cool slightly, about 5 minutes.
