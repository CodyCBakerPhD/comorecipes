Apple Pie Filling

  • American
  • Dessert
  • Pie
  • Vegetarian
  • Fruit

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Peel and core apples; slice thinly.
  2. Toss apples with all dry ingredients except raw sugar.
  3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours.
  4. Drain and reserve liquid in a small saucepan; bring liquid to simmer and reduce by half (stir constantly).
  5. Pour over apples and toss to combine.
  6. Pour into pie crust and seal.
  7. Sprinkle with raw sugar.
  8. Bake to pie crust instructions.
