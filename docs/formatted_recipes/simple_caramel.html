Simple Caramel

  • American
  • Dessert
  • Vegetarian

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Combine condensed milk, butter, brown sugar, and corn syrup in medium saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Stir frequently until butter is melted and ingredients are combined.
  3. Continue to constantly stir until mixture begins to boil.
  4. Once mixture begins to boil, reduce heat to simmer and continue to stir.
  5. Continue for 8-10 minutes until mixture has thickened and darkened.
  6. Remove from heat and immediately mix in vanilla extract and salt.
