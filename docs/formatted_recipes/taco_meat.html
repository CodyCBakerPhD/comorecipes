Taco Meat

Tags: American, Vegetarian, Entree, Spicy


Ingredients

enough avocado oil

453 grams ground beef or refried beans or Boca

24.5 grams taco seasoning

enough water

Instructions

Sear meat until brown.

Add spice and water.

Cook off water to allow base to absorb spice, deglaze the pan, and allow meat to be slightly moist.
