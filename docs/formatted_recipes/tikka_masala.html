Tikka Masala

Tags: Indian, Vegetarian, Entree


Ingredients

1 basmati rice

1 naan

enough peanut oil

250 grams onion

16 grams minced garlic

250 grams onion

33.5 grams tikka masala seasoning

113 grams chickpeas

1 paneer

1 crushed tomatoes

1 coconut milk


Notes

Chickpeas and paneer are interchangeable protein add-ins. Use any amount desired.

Instructions

Make basmati rice.

Heat oil over medium-high.

Saute onion until almost caramelized.

Add garlic and finish caramelizing.

Add seasoning and cook for 1 minute.

Add tomatoes, milk, and chickpeas.

Simmer for 30 minutes.

Add paneer.

Simmer for 10 more minutes.
