Cream Fudge

  • American
  • Dessert
  • Vegetarian
  • Christmas

Ingredients

Notes

  • Constantly stir to avoid any amount of burning.

Instructions

  1. Butter heat-proof pan.
  2. Combine sugar and whole milk into a large saucepan.
  3. Add condensed milk, corn syrup, and butter.
  4. Heat over medium-high heat for approximately 15 minutes, stirring constantly.
  5. Target temperature is 240 F, which should be thick and caramelized.
  6. Remove from heat and carefully transfer to a stand mixer bowl.
  7. Contents will be very hot, so be careful.
  8. Carefully beat with scraper attachment (and shield) on lowest speed until no longer shiny, about 4 minutes.
  9. Pour fudge onto prepared pan and let cool.
