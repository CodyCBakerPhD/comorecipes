Fried Rice

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Make and chill rice a day ahead of time.
  2. Fry tofu at same time.
  3. Scramble eggs and set aside.
  4. Stir fry onion and garlic.
  5. Add other vegetables until cooked.
  6. Add rice and seasonings, fry until ready.
