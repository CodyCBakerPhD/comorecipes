Fried Rice

Tags: Asian, Vegetarian, Entree


Ingredients

1 of chilled rice

1 fried tofu

2 eggs

42 grams butter

15 grams garlic

75 grams white onion

1 carrot

1 Shiitake mushrooms

80 grams frozen or fresh peas

70 grams soy sauce

9/2 grams sesame oil

Instructions

Make and chill rice a day ahead of time.

Fry tofu at same time.

Scramble eggs and set aside.

Stir fry onion and garlic.

Add other vegetables until cooked.

Add rice and seasonings, fry until ready.
