Cobbler Topping

Tags: Component


Ingredients

76 grams butter , room temperature

33 grams sugar

35 grams brown sugar

30 grams flour

0.5 grams baking powder

1 grams salt

1 egg

1 grams vanilla

Instructions

Preheat oven to 375 F.

Cream butter and sugar.

Beat in eggs and vanilla.

Combine remaining dry ingredients.

Gradually add flour mixture.

Drop by spoonfuls over filling.

Bake for 35 minutes or until golden.
