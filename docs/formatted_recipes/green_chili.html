Green Chili

  • Mexican
  • Vegetarian
  • Entree
  • Soup
  • Spicy

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Grill the Anaheim peppers ahead of time; when skin is blackened, place into plastic bag and leave to cool.
  2. Remove skins and set aside.
  3. Dice the onion and Jalapeno peppers.
  4. Heat butter over medium-high heat until foamy, then saute onion.
  5. Add tomatoes, peppers, and spices.
  6. Bring to simmer then lower heat and continue simmering for 15 minutes.
  7. Make a roux with the remaining butter and flour; slowly mix chili into roux while stirring constantly to thicken.
  8. Serve with warm fresh tortillas.
